The backrest is designed to give a few degrees under a casual lean and about twice that under a firm one. Under the 1000 N BIFMA back test, the linear estimate exceeds the yield strength of unfilled polypropylene, so a production shell should use a 20 % talc- or glass-filled PP in the junction (or be tuned with FEA) and be proven by a physical BIFMA X6.1 test. This is normal for moulded school chairs.

## 3. Known limitations

- Anthropometry uses adult US Army data (ANSUR II) as a stand-in; the new YouthShape.US teen survey was not published yet.
- Strength and stiffness numbers come from hand calculations (1-D beams, rigid-body tipping), not FEA. They are good for catching bad ideas early, not for certifying a product.
- The PLA model is a demonstration: it flexes, slides and adjusts like the real design, but PLA fatigues much sooner than polypropylene.
